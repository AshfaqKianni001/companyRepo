Our Story

Runtime Lab started as a small group of students who wanted to build for the web, not just study it.

What began as weekend experiments with HTML and CSS grew into a full team of developers, designers, and media creators delivering real products for real businesses. Today we work across web development, software engineering, IT solutions, and creative media — but the mindset has not changed: ship clean work, measure it honestly, and keep learning.

We are part of TAMZK Group of Companies, and we treat every project as a chance to raise the standard for the next one.
